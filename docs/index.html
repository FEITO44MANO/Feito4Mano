Feito a Mano - Made by hand

This brand is all about handcrafted charm with vintage Americana flair, textured like old film with cozy claymation-style warmth. Inspired by diners, Wes Anderson tones, gingham patterns, and a tomato-palette, Feito a Mano celebrates uniqueness and storytelling in every piece.

F.A.M

F.A.M stands for the community, the spirit of slow craft, and the love behind every design. Whether it's a cherry motif or fine-line tattoo-style drawings, each item is touched with warmth and intention.

Mano

Mano represents the hand that builds, creates, draws, paints, and dreams. This section will highlight behind-the-scenes images and sketches that reflect the journey of handmade work.
